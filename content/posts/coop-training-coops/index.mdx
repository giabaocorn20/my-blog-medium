Hey everyone, part 4 of the co-op diary. This one isn't technical at all. It's about helping new people ramp up, which is weird because I was a new person like five minutes ago ʕ•ᴥ•ʔ

## The informal lead thing

Nobody handed me a "lead" title. It just kind of happens: you've been around a little longer, so the newer co-ops start asking you questions. And suddenly you're the person explaining things. Congratulations, you've been promoted by vibes.

I wasn't ready for it. But it turned out to be some of my favourite work, and it taught me a lot about how I learn too.

## What seemed to help

**1. A small first win.** Something they can finish in a day or two. Confidence matters more than people admit, and one finished thing makes the next one feel possible.

**2. Write it down.** If I explain something out loud, it disappears. If I write it down, the next person gets it for free. Even rough notes beat nothing.

**3. Ask early.** I tell people up front: if you've been stuck for an hour, say so. Nobody is judging the hour. They're judging the three days you didn't mention it. (That's the meme at the top, by the way. Success Kid asked after an hour.)

**4. Teach it back.** Ask them to explain what they just learned to me, or to the next person. If you can explain it, you actually own it. This works on me too.

## What didn't help

- **Dumping everything on day one.** Information overload is real. Nobody remembers a firehose.
- **"Just read the docs."** Docs are great, but they're a lot less friendly when you don't yet know what to look for. Show them where to start first.
- **Doing it for them.** It's faster in the moment and slower forever. I try to explain the "why" and let them type it.

## What I got out of it

Explaining something is the quickest way to find out you don't fully understand it. Several times a question from a newer co-op sent me back to check something I thought I knew (╥﹏╥)

I'm still a student and I'm definitely still learning how to do this well. But if you ever get the chance to help someone ramp up, take it.
